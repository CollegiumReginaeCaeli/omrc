Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli & Sts. Cosmas & Damian Hemophilia Institute

Official Declaration

A Union of Faith and Healing

In the tradition of the holy physician-martyrs Cosmas and Damian, who gave freely of their gifts for the love of God, the Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli hereby enters into Holy Collaboration with the Sts. Cosmas & Damian Hemophilia Institute, uniting prayer, faith, and the apostolate of healthcare in service to those who suffer.

✦ ✦ ✦

Pax et Bonum.

In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.

I, [Name], Custos Generalis of the Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli, by the authority vested in this office and in prayerful confidence in the intercession of Mary, Queen of Heaven, do hereby declare and solemnly proclaim a Holy Collaboration between the Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli and the Sts. Cosmas & Damian Hemophilia Institute.

May this union of purpose, united under the patronage of the Holy Physicians Cosmas and Damian, give glory to God and bring comfort, healing, and hope to all those whom we serve.

Given under my hand and seal this ___ day of ___, in the Year of Our Lord 20___.

In Christo et Maria,

[Name]

The Holy Physicians

Sts. Cosmas & Damian

Cosmas and Damian were twin brothers and physicians, martyred in the early Church for their faith. They became known as the "Anargyroi" — those who practiced medicine without charge — giving freely of their healing gifts as an act of charity and witness. They are among the most beloved of the Church's patron saints of medicine and healing, and their example of selfless service has inspired Catholic healthcare for centuries.

"Saints Cosmas and Damian — holy physicians, martyrs, and servants of the poor — pray for us and for all those we serve." — Prayer of the Order
