Founding Homily

Delivered in Honor of Our Lady, Queen of Heaven

🇮🇹   Italiano

Fratelli e sorelle in Cristo, carissimi membri dell'Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli — soldati della Regina del Cielo — vi saluto nel nome del Padre, del Figlio e dello Spirito Santo. Siamo riuniti oggi non per caso, ma per una Provvidenza divina che ci ha chiamati, in questi tempi oscuri e confusi, a rialzare il vessillo della Fede immacolata, a inginocchiarci davanti al trono della Santissima Vergine Maria, e a difendere con cuore intrepido le verità eterne che la Santa Chiesa ci ha trasmesso attraverso i secoli. Maria, Regina del Cielo e della Terra, Stella Maris, guida delle anime perdute, è la nostra patrona, la nostra madre, la nostra forza. Sotto il suo manto cerchiamo rifugio. Sotto il suo stendardo marceremo, senza paura, verso la Gerusalemme Celeste.

🇪🇸   Español

Hermanos y hermanas, soldados de la Reina del Cielo: los saludo con el corazón lleno de gratitud y de esperanza. En un mundo que ha perdido el rumbo, que ha cambiado la verdad eterna por la novedad pasajera, nosotros elegimos el camino antiguo — la senda antigua, como nos dice el profeta Jeremías: "Paraos en los caminos y mirad, y preguntad por las sendas antiguas cuál es el buen camino, y andad por él, y hallaréis descanso para vuestras almas." (Jer. 6:16). María, nuestra Reina, nunca cambia. Su amor es eterno, su intercesión es poderosa, y su corona de doce estrellas brilla hoy tan radiante como el día en que San Juan la contempló en el Apocalipsis. Que ella nos guíe, que ella nos proteja, y que el manto de su gracia cubra a todos los miembros de esta Orden, ahora y siempre.

🇺🇸   English

On Our Lady, Queen of Heaven

Brothers and sisters, we have greeted one another in Italian and in Spanish — in the tongues of Rome and of the great Marian nations — because devotion to Our Lady knows no border, no language, no age. It is as old as the Annunciation itself. It is as universal as the Catholic Faith.

We who belong to the Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli bear her name upon our lips and upon our seal. Reginae Caeli — Queen of Heaven. These are not merely beautiful words. They are a declaration. A proclamation. A battle standard planted firmly in the ground of eternity.

Who is this Queen? She is the woman of the Apocalypse, clothed with the sun, the moon beneath her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars. She is the Theotokos, the God-bearer, proclaimed at Ephesus in 431. She is the Immaculate Conception, defined by Blessed Pius IX in 1854. She is the Queen assumed body and soul into Heaven, as Pius XII solemnly defined in Munificentissimus Deus in 1950. She is not a legend. She is not a symbol. She is a person, a mother, our mother, reigning in glory at the right hand of her Divine Son.

"The salvation of the world began through Mary and through Mary it must be accomplished." — St. Louis de Montfort

On Our Co-Patron, Venerable Fulton J. Sheen

We turn to our beloved Co-Patron — that great son of the American Church, that titan of the Faith, that voice crying in the wilderness of the twentieth century: the Venerable Fulton J. Sheen. Archbishop Sheen understood, perhaps better than any man of his age, that the crisis of the modern world was not political, not economic, not social — it was spiritual. It was a crisis of souls. And he spent every breath God gave him fighting for those souls.

He stood before the cameras of a skeptical, secular nation and spoke the truths of the Catholic Faith with clarity, with charity, and with a wit sharper than any sword. He spoke of sin when the world denied it. He spoke of redemption when the world had forgotten it needed saving. He spoke of Our Lady when even some within the Church had grown embarrassed by her.

Indeed, Fulton Sheen had a profound and tender devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary. He attributed every grace of his priesthood and his apostolate to her intercession. He was ordained a priest on the Feast of the Immaculate Conception and renewed that consecration every single day of his priestly life until his last breath in 1979.

He is our Co-Patron because he is the model of what it means to be a militant Catholic in the modern age — not militant with weapons of steel, but with weapons of prayer, of preaching, of the Sacraments, of truth spoken with love. He feared no one but God. He compromised nothing. And he won countless souls for Christ through His Mother.

"Venerable Fulton J. Sheen — pray for us." — Prayer of the Order

On Holy Tradition and the Ancient Ways of the Church

There is one more pillar upon which this Order stands, and we must speak of it plainly and without apology: our fidelity to Holy Tradition. To the ancient ways of the Church. To the Mass, the Sacraments, the prayers, the theology, the piety — the whole patrimony of Catholic civilization as it was handed down to us from the Apostles, through the Fathers, through the great Councils, through the saints and martyrs, through a hundred generations of faithful Catholics who suffered and died so that we might receive the Faith whole and intact.

The Traditional Latin Mass — the Missa of St. Gregory the Great, the Mass of the Roman Rite as it was codified by St. Pius V in 1570, carried across oceans by missionaries, whispered in prisons by martyrs — is not an antiquarian curiosity. It is a living treasury. It is the highest act of worship the human race has ever offered to Almighty God.

Pope St. Pius X — that great anti-Modernist pontiff, whose battle cry was "Instaurare omnia in Christo" — to restore all things in Christ — is our example. We, too, love souls. And because we love them, we will not dilute the Faith. We will not trade the eternal for the fashionable. We will not exchange the chant of the angels for the applause of the world.

Closing — Sub tuum Praesidium

Brothers and sisters, soldiers of the Queen of Heaven — we live in extraordinary times. The world is in confusion. Even within the Church there is much darkness and uncertainty. But we must not be afraid. Our Lady herself appeared at Fatima in 1917 and told us what was coming, and she told us also what would save us: the Rosary, penance, consecration, Tradition, faithfulness.

She is our Queen. Fulton Sheen is our patron and our model. And the Faith of our fathers is our treasure and our sword.

Let us go forth from this place renewed in our commitment — to pray without ceasing, to defend the Faith without apology, to love Our Lady without reserve, and to stand firm in the ancient ways until that day when the Queen of Heaven places upon our brow the crown she has kept for her faithful soldiers from all eternity.

Sub tuum praesidium confugimus, Sancta Dei Genetrix.
Under thy protection we take refuge, O Holy Mother of God.

Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli — ora et pugna.
The Order of the Warriors of the Queen of Heaven — pray and fight. — Deo gratias. Amen.
