Our Online Shop is Coming

The Collegium Reginae Caeli is preparing its online shop, where you will soon be able to purchase sacred art, devotional materials, catechetical texts, Traditional liturgical resources, and publications produced by the Order — all crafted in the spirit of the perennial Catholic Faith and offered under the patronage of Our Lady, Queen of Heaven.

✦ ✦ ✦

What to Expect

✥

Sacred Art & Prints

Devotional images, holy cards, and sacred prints in the Traditional Catholic style.

✝

Catechetical Materials

Books, booklets, and resources for the formation of the faithful in the Traditional Faith.

✶

Marian Devotionals

Rosary guides, scapular materials, Marian consecration texts, and novenas.

❧

OMRC Publications

Official publications, prayer cards, and materials produced by the Order.
