The Constitution is Being Prepared

The founding documents and constitution of the Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli are currently being drafted by the Custos Generalis and the founding members of the Order. When ratified, the full text of the Constitution will be published here for all members and the faithful to read.

The Constitution will set forth the Rule of Life, the rights and duties of members, the governance of the Order, its apostolic aims, and the spiritual foundations upon which the OMRC is built — all in fidelity to the perennial Magisterium of the Church and the Traditional Roman Rite.
