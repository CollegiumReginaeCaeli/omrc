Our Co-Patron

Venerable Fulton J. Sheen

A Voice in the Wilderness

Venerable Fulton J. Sheen

The Order claims as its Co-Patron the Venerable Fulton J. Sheen — that great son of the American Church, that titan of the Faith, that voice crying in the wilderness of the twentieth century.

Archbishop Sheen understood, perhaps better than any man of his age, that the crisis of the modern world was not political, not economic, not social — it was spiritual. It was a crisis of souls. And he spent every breath God gave him fighting for those souls.

He stood before the cameras of a skeptical, secular nation and spoke the truths of the Catholic Faith with clarity, with charity, and with a wit sharper than any sword. He spoke of sin when the world denied it. He spoke of redemption when the world had forgotten it needed saving. He spoke of Our Lady when even some within the Church had grown embarrassed by her.

A Marian Priest

Everything Through His Mother

Sheen had a profound and tender devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary. He attributed every grace of his priesthood and apostolate to her intercession. He was ordained on the Feast of the Immaculate Conception and renewed his consecration to Our Lady every single day of his priestly life.

He made a Holy Hour before the Blessed Sacrament every day for more than sixty years, and said that everything he ever accomplished came out of it. He feared no one but God. He compromised nothing. And he won countless souls for Christ through His Mother.

"Venerable Fulton J. Sheen — pray for us." — Prayer of the Order
Why He Is Our Co-Patron

The Model We Carry Into Every Battle

He is our Co-Patron because he is the living proof that a militant Catholic in the modern age need carry no weapon but truth, spoken with love and backed by prayer. He fought with a microphone, a pen, and a Holy Hour, and he was more dangerous to the enemy than any army.

He is our Co-Patron because he united in one life everything this Order aspires to: the healing of souls, the teaching of the Faith, fidelity to Holy Tradition, and a life formed around Our Lady. His cause is advanced; we pray for the day the Church raises him to the altars.
