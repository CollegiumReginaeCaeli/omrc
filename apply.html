Before You Apply

Membership Requirements

The Ordo Militantium Reginae Caeli is a Catholic Militant Order dedicated to the defense and proclamation of the Traditional Faith. Membership is a serious commitment — a calling to serve Our Lady, Holy Mother Church, and those most in need, through prayer, sacrifice, and apostolic work.

All applicants must:

  • Be a practicing Catholic in good standing with Holy Mother Church.
  • Be at least 18 years of age at the time of application.
  • Hold a sincere devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary and a commitment to Traditional Catholic piety.
  • Be willing to abide by the Rule of Life and spirit of the Order as directed by the Custos Generalis.
  • Commit to the Order's apostolates in healthcare, education, the Faith, and fraternity according to their ability and vocation.
✦ ✦ ✦
"Sub tuum praesidium confugimus, Sancta Dei Genetrix."
Under thy protection we take refuge, O Holy Mother of God. — Motto of the Order

Applications are reviewed by the Custos Generalis. You will be contacted following the review of your submission. All information provided is treated with the utmost discretion.
