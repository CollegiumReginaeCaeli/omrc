We Welcome You

Whether you wish to inquire about membership, our apostolates, the Collegium Reginae Caeli, or anything else pertaining to the Order, we are glad to hear from you.

All who seek to serve under the mantle of Our Lady, Queen of Heaven, are welcome to reach out. We will endeavor to respond to all inquiries with care and in a timely manner.
